# FAQ

## Product

### What is AI WONDERLAND?

AI WONDERLAND combines WonderBuild, AI Playground, NPC/3D tools, projects, usage/billing and related creation surfaces under one account.

### Which AI models are supported?

There is no single permanent model list. AI Playground can expose enabled OpenRouter and direct-provider model routes configured by the platform. Availability can change as providers, pricing and safety controls change.

Builders do not currently expose three selectable AI modes everywhere. **Fast, Balanced and Advanced are planned future builder choices.**

### Does AI WONDERLAND own every foundation model it uses?

No. AI WONDERLAND owns its product layer, orchestration, agents, billing/usage controls and custom workflows. Underlying model inference can come from providers such as OpenRouter-routed models, Google or other configured providers.

### How does WonderBuild work?

WonderBuild provides the visual website/app creation flow. AI-assisted generation and editing should only be described as available where the corresponding route is enabled and metered.

## Billing

### What plans are available?

The six customer-facing memberships are **Nomad, Creator, Architect, Studio, Guild and Enterprise**.

### Is AI Playground a separate subscription?

No. It is a product area inside the AI WONDERLAND membership.

### What are AI credits?

AI credits are normalized billing units across platform-funded AI. AI Playground can still show the selected model/provider. More expensive model classes use credits faster.

### Are 3D credits separate?

Yes. 3D generation/rendering has its own credit balance.

### Can I buy more usage without upgrading?

Yes, where purchase packs are configured. AI credits and 3D credits can be sold as one-time add-ons without changing the membership.

### Does returning from Stripe mean my plan is active?

No. The membership becomes authoritative only after the Stripe event is verified and the subscription state is stored successfully.

## WonderSpace

### Is the cloud IDE generally available?

Not yet. Customer WonderSpace provisioning is a **controlled beta** and remains behind isolation, provisioning and cost-control gates. A plan does not guarantee immediate workspace creation while those gates are disabled.

## Security and data

### Do you store prompts or outputs?

Retention depends on the product surface and enabled integrations. Do not assume every prompt or output has the same retention policy. Account-specific deletion or retention controls should be described only where they are implemented.

### Is my data encrypted?

AI WONDERLAND relies on configured hosting/database providers and HTTPS/TLS for supported production traffic. Specific encryption-at-rest guarantees depend on the active provider/service configuration and should not be represented more broadly than those provider guarantees.

### How do I report a security problem?

Use the support/security contact shown on the live AI WONDERLAND site. Do not include passwords, secret keys or other credentials in a report.

## Deployment and publishing

### Can I publish a WonderBuild project?

Publishing should be described as available only for the routes and providers that are production-enabled. Export and preview capabilities are separate from managed publishing.

### Are custom domains available on every paid plan?

Do not assume so from an old pricing table. The current plan catalog should be treated as authoritative, and domain entitlements should only be advertised after the matching production path is verified.

## Troubleshooting

### Checkout says setup pending

That plan/interval does not yet have a verified live Stripe Product/Price configuration available to the application.

### I hit an AI usage limit

Open the usage/billing area to review included and purchased AI credits. A higher-cost model can consume credits faster than a standard model.

### I hit a 3D limit

3D uses a separate credit balance. Review included/purchased 3D credits rather than the AI-credit meter.

### WonderSpace will not create a workspace

Customer workspace creation may be paused by the controlled-beta safety gates. This is different from the Coder control plane itself being offline.
