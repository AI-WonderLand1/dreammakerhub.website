# Code of Conduct

AI WONDERLAND INNOVATION wants project spaces to be useful, professional, and safe for contributors, users, reviewers, and maintainers.

This code applies to GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, project-managed community spaces, and other collaboration channels operated for DreamMakerHub.website.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, and outcomes rather than people;
- be patient with contributors who have different backgrounds, experience levels, or primary languages;
- give actionable technical feedback and accept reasonable review feedback in good faith;
- respect privacy and never publish another person's private information without permission;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive behavior;
- follow repository security, contribution, and licensing requirements.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- personal attacks, slurs, discriminatory language, or targeted harassment;
- threats of violence or encouragement of harm;
- publishing or threatening to publish private identifying information;
- repeated unwanted contact or sexual attention;
- deliberately sabotaging collaboration, reviews, issues, or community spaces;
- posting credentials, private customer data, or exploit details in public channels after being directed to a private security-reporting path.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Report community or conduct concerns privately to **contact@dreammakerhub.website**. Include enough context to understand what occurred, but do not include unrelated private information.

For software vulnerabilities or security-sensitive reports, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting when available or email **security@dreammakerhub.website** instead. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community. Decisions should be based on documented behavior and applied as consistently as practical.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment is not.
