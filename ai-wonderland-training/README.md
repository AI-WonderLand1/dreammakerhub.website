# AI Wonderland supervised training starter (direct-download edition)

Includes 60 training and 16 independent held-out evaluation chat-message JSONL records across TypeScript, Python, C++/3D, AI Confessions, and WonderSpace agent design.

Download the .jsonl files with curl or use GitHub's Raw button. Import `ai_wonderland_train.jsonl` into Unsloth Studio in chat-messages format. Keep `ai_wonderland_eval.jsonl` separate. These are a compact independently generated replacement for the chat ZIP that did not download; they are not a byte-for-byte copy of that ZIP. Review all examples before training. Do not train on secrets or personal customer data. Your CPU-only HP with 8 GB RAM is better for model inference and data preparation than fine-tuning.
